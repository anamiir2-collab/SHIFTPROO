## 2. Store listing — English (en)

**App title (max 30 chars):** ShiftPro

**Short description (max 80 chars):**
Offline shifts, attendance, working hours and salary manager.

**Full description (max 4000 chars):**
```
ShiftPro is a complete, offline-first companion for managing your work shifts,
attendance, working hours and salary. Designed for individual employees and
small teams, it puts every record you need on a single device — no account, no
server, no cloud sync.

KEY FEATURES

• Shifts and schedules
  Define shift templates by code, name, start/end time and color. Assign
  shifts to any day, override the schedule per-date, and see at a glance who
  works when.

• Attendance
  One-tap check-in and check-out with automatic working-hours calculation.
  Overnight shifts that cross midnight are handled correctly. Edit any past
  day, add notes, and correct mistakes without losing history.

• Salary calculation
  Monthly, hourly and per-shift salary modes. Overtime, bonuses, allowances,
  official-holiday pay and deductions are computed automatically from the
  recorded working hours. Every salary component is shown transparently.

• Calendar
  A color-coded month view of attendance status, scheduled shifts, leave days
  and official holidays. Navigate any month, tap any day for full details.

• Reports and export
  Generate reports for any date range. Export to PDF, Excel (XLSX), CSV or
  iCalendar. Reports are fully bilingual (Arabic and English).

• Leaves
  Track annual, casual, sick and custom leave types. Carry-forward balances
  into the next period. Half-day and by-hours leaves supported.

• Backup and restore
  Export a full JSON backup of every setting and record. Restore on the same
  or a different device. Your data never leaves your device unless you
  explicitly export it.

• Arabic and English
  Full RTL and LTR interface, switchable at any time. All reports, charts and
  exports respect the active language.

• Works fully offline
  Every feature works without an internet connection. No analytics, no
  telemetry, no remote servers.

PRIVACY

All attendance, salary and working-hours data is stored locally on your
device. ShiftPro does not transmit your records to any server. The optional
Google Fonts link (for typography) degrades gracefully to system fonts when
offline. Review the full policy at:
https://anamiir2-collab.github.io/SHIFTPROO/privacy-policy.html
```

## 11. Screenshots — MANUAL STEP

The Play Console requires 2 to 8 phone screenshots per supported language
(min 320 px, max 3840 px; 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio). These must reflect
REAL app screens — do NOT fabricate screenshots of features that do not exist.

### Recommended screenshots to capture (run the app on an Android emulator or device, then capture):
1. Dashboard (home) — shows live clock, today's shift, quick stats
2. Attendance check-in screen — shows check-in / check-out buttons
3. Calendar month view — shows color-coded attendance status
4. Salary breakdown card — shows salary components (base, overtime, etc.)
5. Reports screen — shows the date range + summary
6. Settings sheet — shows language switch, themes
7. Leave balances card — shows leave types and remaining days
8. About sheet — shows privacy policy link

### How to capture on emulator/device:
```sh
# After installing the debug APK on an emulator:
adb shell screencap -p /sdcard/shiftpro-01.png
adb pull /sdcard/shiftpro-01.png screenshots/shiftpro-01.png
# Repeat for each screen.
```

Screenshots should be captured in **both** Arabic (RTL primary) and English
for the two store listings.

## 12. Account verification & testing (Play Console)

For a brand-new developer account (post-Aug-2023 policy):

1. **Phone verification** — done during account creation.
2. **Identity verification** — government ID + face match required.
3. **Testing requirements** for new personal accounts:
   - Run a closed testing track with ≥20 testers who opt in and stay opted-in
     for 14 days continuously before you can submit to production.
   - Or, for organization accounts, run an internal test track (no minimum
     testers).
4. **App bundle** — must be in `.aab` format (NOT `.apk`). The CI workflow
   produces this.

> Verify the current tester-count and duration requirements on the official
> Play Console Help pages (cited below) at the time of submission, since
> Google updates these policies periodically.
